## IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT YOUR EIN

### Keep This Notice
Keep this notice in your permanent records. This notice is issued only one time. The IRS will not reissue this notice unless you submit a written request and pay the required fee.

### Use Your EIN
You must use your EIN on all tax forms, tax returns, and other documents sent to the IRS and the Social Security Administration. You may also need to give your EIN to third parties who must include it on forms sent to the IRS.

### Tax Obligations
The type of tax return you must file is based on the type of entity you selected on Form SS-4. Here's what applies to you:

**Entity Type:** Corporation
**Tax Form:** Form 1120, U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return
**Filing Frequency:** Annual

### Other Requirements
- **Estimated Tax:** If applicable, use Form 1120-W to compute estimated tax
- **Employment Taxes:** If you have employees, file Form 941 quarterly
- **Information Returns:** File Form W-2 for employees annually
