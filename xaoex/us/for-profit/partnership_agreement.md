# PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT
# XAOEX PARTNERSHIP

---

**This Partnership Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into as of the ____ day of __________, 20__ ("Effective Date"), by and among the undersigned partners (collectively, the "Partners").

---

## ARTICLE 1: FORMATION AND NAME

**1.1 Formation.** The Partners hereby form a general partnership (the "Partnership") under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**1.2 Name.** The name of the Partnership shall be **XAOEX Partnership** or such other name as the Partners may from time to time designate.

**1.3 Principal Place of Business.** The principal place of business of the Partnership shall be:
```
1013 Centre Road
Wilmington, Delaware 19805
```

**1.4 Term.** The Partnership shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until dissolved in accordance with this Agreement or by operation of law.

---

## ARTICLE 2: PURPOSE AND BUSINESS

**2.1 Purpose.** The purpose of the Partnership is to engage in the following business activities:
- Technology development and innovation
- Software development and services
- Business consulting and advisory services
- Any other lawful business activity agreed upon by the Partners

**2.2 Powers.** The Partnership shall have all powers necessary or convenient to carry out its purpose and conduct its business.

---

**3.2 Additional Contributions.** Additional capital contributions may be required by unanimous consent of the Partners.

**3.3 No Interest on Capital.** No Partner shall be entitled to interest on their capital contribution.

**3.4 Capital Accounts.** An individual capital account shall be maintained for each Partner.

---

## ARTICLE 4: PROFITS, LOSSES, AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**4.1 Allocation of Profits and Losses.** Profits and losses shall be allocated among the Partners in proportion to their respective partnership interests as follows:

| Partner Name | Partnership Interest |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Partner 1    | [%]                 |
| Partner 2    | [%]                 |
| Partner 3    | [%]                 |
| **TOTAL**    | **100%**           |

**4.2 Distributions.** Distributions shall be made at such times and in such amounts as determined by majority vote of the Partners.

**4.3 Limitation on Distributions.** No distribution shall be made if it would violate applicable law or render the Partnership insolvent.

---

## ARTICLE 5: MANAGEMENT AND VOTING

**5.1 Management Authority.** All Partners shall have equal rights in the management and conduct of the Partnership business.

**5.2 Voting.** Unless otherwise specified in this Agreement, all decisions shall be made by majority vote of the Partners based on their partnership interests.

**5.3 Major Decisions.** The following actions require unanimous consent of all Partners:
- Amendment of this Agreement
- Admission of new partners
- Sale or disposition of substantially all Partnership assets
- Dissolution of the Partnership
- Merger or consolidation
- Incurrence of debt exceeding $[Amount]

**5.4 Day-to-Day Operations.** Any Partner may make decisions regarding day-to-day operations within the ordinary course of business.

---

## ARTICLE 6: DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS

**6.1 Duty of Care.** Each Partner shall discharge their duties in good faith, with the care an ordinarily prudent person would exercise in similar circumstances.

**6.2 Duty of Loyalty.** Partners shall account to the Partnership for any benefit derived from Partnership activities and refrain from competing with the Partnership.

**6.3 Time and Attention.** Each Partner shall devote such time and attention to Partnership business as is reasonably necessary.

**6.4 Expenses.** The Partnership shall reimburse Partners for reasonable expenses incurred on behalf of the Partnership.

---

## ARTICLE 7: BOOKS, RECORDS, AND ACCOUNTING

**7.1 Books and Records.** The Partnership shall maintain complete and accurate books and records at its principal place of business.

**7.2 Accounting Method.** The Partnership shall use the accrual method of accounting.

**7.3 Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year of the Partnership shall end on December 31.

**7.4 Tax Returns.** The Partnership shall file all required federal, state, and local tax returns.

**7.5 Access to Records.** Each Partner shall have access to all Partnership books and records during normal business hours.

---

## ARTICLE 8: TRANSFER OF PARTNERSHIP INTERESTS

**8.1 Restriction on Transfer.** No Partner may transfer, sell, assign, or otherwise dispose of their partnership interest without the prior written consent of all other Partners.

**8.2 Right of First Refusal.** If a Partner wishes to transfer their interest, the other Partners shall have a right of first refusal to purchase the interest on the same terms.

**8.3 Admission of New Partners.** New partners may be admitted only by unanimous consent of all existing Partners.

---

## ARTICLE 9: WITHDRAWAL AND DISSOLUTION

**9.1 Voluntary Withdrawal.** A Partner may withdraw from the Partnership upon [60] days' written notice to all other Partners.

**9.2 Events of Dissolution.** The Partnership shall dissolve upon:
- Unanimous agreement of all Partners
- Withdrawal, death, bankruptcy, or incapacity of a Partner (unless remaining Partners agree to continue)
- Any event making it unlawful to continue the Partnership
- Judicial decree

**9.3 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Partnership shall wind up its affairs, liquidate assets, and distribute proceeds in the following order:
1. Payment of Partnership debts and obligations
2. Payment to Partners for loans and advances
3. Return of capital contributions
4. Distribution of remaining assets according to partnership interests

---

## ARTICLE 10: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**10.1 Mediation.** Disputes shall first be submitted to mediation.

**10.2 Arbitration.** If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

**10.3 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

---

## ARTICLE 11: MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among the Partners.

**11.2 Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by written agreement signed by all Partners.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision is found invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.

**11.4 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses listed in this Agreement.

**11.5 Binding Effect.** This Agreement shall bind and benefit the Partners and their respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns.
