## TAX OBLIGATIONS

As a C-Corporation, your organization:

1. **Must file Form 1120** (U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return) annually
2. **Is subject to corporate income tax** on taxable income
3. **May elect S-Corporation status** by filing Form 2553 if eligible
4. **Must maintain corporate formalities** and proper record-keeping

### Filing Requirements:
- **Annual Tax Return:** Form 1120 due by the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end
- **Estimated Taxes:** Quarterly (if required) using Form 1120-W
- **Employment Taxes:** Forms 941, 940, W-2, W-3 (if you have employees)

---

## IMPORTANT INFORMATION

### Corporate Tax Rate
Under current tax law, C-Corporations are subject to a flat 21% federal corporate income tax rate on taxable income.

### Dividend Taxation
Dividends paid to shareholders are not deductible by the corporation and are taxable to shareholders as dividend income.

### Alternative Elections
If eligible, you may elect S-Corporation status by filing Form 2553 within the required time frame. S-Corporations are pass-through entities with different tax treatment.

### Record Retention
Maintain all corporate records, including:
- Articles of Incorporation
- Bylaws and amendments
- Board meeting minutes
- Shareholder records
- Financial statements
- Tax returns and supporting documents
